About Rated420

Rated420 gives New York cannabis consumers a place to record what they tried, compare experiences and discover what to explore next. A shared scorecard makes it easier to compare perspectives while leaving room for individual preferences.

How the 420-point score works

Each review evaluates five dimensions. Every dimension carries the same weight, with 0 to 84 points available. Their sum gives a total from 0 to 420.

  • Aesthetic 84
  • Aroma 84
  • Flavor or consistency 84
  • Effects 84
  • Overall 84

S = x₁ + x₂ + x₃ + x₄ + x₅

Flavor applies when taste is part of the experience. For topicals, the third category is Consistency. Dispensaries use Aesthetic, Accessibility, Staff, Menu and Overall. Brands use Presentation, Consistency, Selection, Transparency and Overall. Accessories use Design, Build quality, Ease of use, Performance and Overall. All use the same 420-point scale.

Community averages

A = round((S₁ + S₂ + … + Sₙ) ÷ n)

We average the reviewer scores at full precision and round only the displayed result. For strain listings, each reviewer contributes equally. Flower and other product formats can be viewed separately. In All formats, a reviewer’s eligible reviews are averaged together before they count as one contribution. With no reviews, the listing is unrated. The numbers summarize personal experiences.
