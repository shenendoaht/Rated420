# Rated420 scoring and About

Use **About → About Rated420** for the company introduction, individual and community equations, category guide and independent calculator. The homepage retains its scoring overview and the 420 Standard. The review form puts the live total just before its sliders and keeps a compact link to dispensary ratings.

The display utility rounds valid numeric values to the nearest integer. Aggregation, format filters, reviewer deduplication, sort order, exports and stored values keep their original precision. Zero scores are valid. Listings without reviews remain unrated. The calculator has no submission or persistence. Topical uses Consistency in the third position. It still maps to `flavor` in storage, and the Stash keeps its existing five-element score array.

The unified sandbox filters out only the personal accessory fixture IDs `houseplant-grinder` and `puffco`. Earlier browser-local data is preserved, including stored reviews, other user-created listings and favorites. Houseplant Glass Grinder, Puffco Peak and the other editorial products remain in the manufacturer showcase.

To launch the ZIP, extract it and use **Launch Rated420.command** on macOS or run `/usr/bin/perl ./serve-rated420.pl` in the extracted directory. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8768/`. The browser retains its existing demo data for that origin.
