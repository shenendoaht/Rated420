# Rated420 Founder focused workspace

Baseline `87c72ab8de8d1454f307b859a8736ef68c834d5c`. The prior sandbox is retained for rollback.

Founder opens on Needs attention. Open follow-ups and correspondence directly. Pending catalogue proposals or simulated intake stay prominent when present. Pipeline and production, plus recent activity, open on demand.

Founder navigation starts closed. Open it to select a destination from Today, Relationships, Intelligence or Operations, then choose Open. This replaces the separate area and destination controls. Back to Rated420 HQ and Demo settings are in the same panel. Escape closes it and restores focus. The existing finance, territory, data intelligence, relationship, correspondence, product, campaign, document and production editors keep their routes, role boundaries and data adapters. Within Founder, opening a related record preserves the originating record or filtered list for Back. Staff HQ retains its separate administrative workspace.

The About team story crops only the original bundled Treehouse photo display. The crop shows all three team members and removes the storefront sign from the frame. Custom editorial photos retain their normal framing. The original asset is still used by the homepage and catalogue.

To run offline, extract the ZIP and open **Launch Rated420.command** on macOS, or run `/usr/bin/perl ./serve-rated420.pl` from the extracted folder and open `http://127.0.0.1:8768/`.
